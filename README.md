### Game Details & Guide
About Ballistic Chickens


Ballistic Chickens is a browser game developed by Gameloft, released in 2019. The game is set on a dynamic terrain where players must navigate their chicken characters to survive against enemies and collect eggs. The objective of the game is to score points by reaching the end of each level while avoiding obstacles.


How to Play


To play Ballistic Chickens, you need to use your mouse to control the direction of movement for your chicken character. You can also use the WASD keys or arrow keys to make the character move in different directions.

The game features a variety of challenges and levels with increasing difficulty. Players must navigate their chicken through obstacles such as rocks, pits, and other hazards. The eggs scattered throughout the levels are worth points and must be collected to increase your score.


Tips and Tricks for Success


Here are some tips and tricks to help you succeed in Ballistic Chickens:
• Learn the map: Familiarize yourself with the terrain and obstacles before starting a level.
• Use your resources wisely: Collect as many eggs as possible, but avoid wasting them on unnecessary items.
• Avoid taking damage: Keep moving and stay close to your chicken to avoid getting hit by enemies or falling off the map.
• Stay alert: Pay attention to your surroundings at all times to avoid accidents.
